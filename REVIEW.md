## Visual pass

- Triplanar normal maps: the old `brgTriplanarNormal` read the z component of all three plane samples as world z. On a roof (up-facing) the result tilted the normal toward z, so every roof was shaded as if it faced one way. It now uses the whiteout blend (swizzled per plane). Before and after screenshots of the bridge district show shingle relief on roofs that was missing before. Walls that face along z were already right.
- Colour spaces checked and left alone: albedo maps are `SRGBColorSpace`, normal and roughness maps are `NoColorSpace`, `TextureLoader` textures keep mipmaps, anisotropy is 8 on the material maps. I did not set anisotropy from `getMaxAnisotropy()`.
- SSAO: the noise seed used `vUv * 147`, which varies slowly and left bands on flat ground. It now uses `gl_FragCoord`, and the 5x5 blur averages it out.
- Default view: it was 24 degrees above the horizon. At 16 degrees the sky haze and the hills show, as in the OG picture, and the mean luminance of the map area rises from 94 to 106 (live before: 87, OG hero: 125). I did not change the fog density: the haze looks right at 16 degrees, and the OG hero uses a different camera, so a density change would not make the two views comparable.
- Not done: LOD with hysteresis, KTX2 textures and a sky panorama. The sky is procedural and I saw no seam, so I left it.

## Known limits

- Evidence for the bridge is from SIPA text (found by web search; the SIPA page itself did not load): five unequal arches, taller in the middle, four cutwaters upstream, four buttresses downstream, about 100 m long. The span widths (12, 16, 20, 17, 13 m) and the 3.6 m piers are estimates. The real arches are pointed and the cutwaters project about 2 m. The model has segmental arches under the 8 m height and cutwaters that project 0.35 m, because the fit rule allows 10 % over the OSM extent. The "upstream = east" direction is inferred from geography (the Cávado flows west), not read from data.
- `torre-menagem` is 20 m, not 24 m: a search-indexed text says "cerca de 20 metros", and the photo scales to about 19 m. `dimensions.json` carries the source and `confidence: medium`. The page itself did not load in the fetch tool. The gate side (OSM edge at 21 degrees) is an assumption.
- `pacos-concelho`: the east arcade edge is the front by the photos and the street layout; no survey fixes the bearing. The clock tower position and the second tower are read from two photos. 22 k triangles is above the 6 to 15 k target, and the arcade, balconies and merlons account for it.
- `igreja-barcelinhos` is still an archetype with a generic front. Its photo shows a baroque church with two bell towers and a curved gable. It is the next candidate. The file `assets/img/igreja-barcelinhos.jpg` shows the Capela da Ponte; the content uses `-1.jpg`, so nothing reads the stray file.
- `road.bridge_models` is a Barcelos extension of `road-network.js` and `roads.js`. Braga and the other forks do not have it. Port it with the next engine sync, or drop it.
- The ruin of `paco-condes` sits lower than the church (separate flattening pads).
- Only two of the five newer story camera presets were checked by screenshot.
- Share pages and the sitemap use `barcelos-3d.vercel.app` (`public_url`) while `barcelos-3d.com` does not resolve. Switch `public_url` and re-run `node scripts/make-og.mjs --city barcelos --pages` when the domain is live.
- hreflang points at `?lang=` URLs under a root canonical, which search engines may treat as a mismatch. Porto has no hreflang.
- Candidates without a landmark: `senhor-galo` (the city's brand symbol), `largo-municipio`, `sao-francisco`, `mosteiro-terco`, `igreja-santa-cruz`, `franqueira`. They are listed only in `cities/barcelos.json` `landmark_candidates`; their specs were removed from `src/models.js`.
- No adaptive performance governor (porto-3d has one). SSAO runs only inside the post-processing composer, which is off on phones and light mode.
- Right after load on a CDN, distant buildings show a stipple pattern for a few seconds while tiles fade in. It is gone after 30 s (checked on the live site).
- Storage keys and the debug global still use the engine's `braga-` and `__braga` names. Renaming them across all forks is a joint step.
- FPS and SSAO cost could not be measured: the Apple GPU is capped at 60 fps.
- `sync-engine.mjs` against porto-3d (read-only, `ENGINE_BASE=1a77430`): same 20, behind 29, ahead 9, diverged 36. Porto is far ahead of this fork in `water.js`, `weather.js`, `textures.js`, `road-structures.js` and the check scripts; most shared files diverge, so a port needs a manual merge.
